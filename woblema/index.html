About Us

A Ghanaian cultural NGO with a big heart

Woblema Heritage Cultural Troupe LBG is a leading Ghanaian cultural NGO committed to the preservation and global promotion of Ghana's intangible cultural heritage.

The name "Woblema" comes from the Ga language and means "History of our fathers". It reflects our mission: to bring the soul of Ghana to audiences near and far.

Founded in 2017 by a Ghanaian businessman and a musician, Woblema began as a grassroots community ensemble. Today it is a 35-member professional troupe of master drummers, traditional dancers, vocalists and folk instrumentalists. We are a non-profit, Limited by Guarantee, based in Accra, Ghana.

What We Do

Social Impact

Growing the next generation

Beyond performance, our social impact lies in youth development. Through our NGO arm, we operate four programs.

Woblema Youth Academy

Weekly training in traditional music and dance for youth aged 12-25.

Schools Heritage Program

Bringing cultural education into basic schools.

Women in Culture Initiative

Training young women in cultural performance and costume design.

Instrument Craft Workshop

Apprenticeship in making traditional drums like the Fontomfrom, Atumpan and Kpanlogo drums.

The Face of Woblema

Michael Bampoe Addo aka G-WASS

From traditional dancer, to world tour, to music video director. Today he is the face of Woblema Heritage Cultural Troupe.

"Music found me after dance, then I found the lens."
- G-WASS
1998

Joins Talented Youth Theatre (T.Y.T)

G-WASS becomes a traditional dancer and joins the culture troupe Talented Youth Theatre.

2001

First trip to Ireland

Travelling with Kake Dance Assembly, a collaboration with T.Y.T, the group toured Callan, Listowel, Cashel, Bantry and Cork.

2003

T.Y.T becomes Tessa Cultural Troupe

Tessa became an iconic cultural troupe in Ghana, featured in music videos and mixing traditional dance with contemporary music.

2006

Luton Carnival, UK

A carnival performance in the UK, followed by trips to Croatia and Liberia. Tessa has performed in Dubai, Saudi Arabia, America and beyond.

Music

4.2Life and the hit "Iskoki"

He formed the music group 4.2Life, whose hit song "Iskoki" crossed borders.

Now

Director & face of Woblema

He moved into music video directing and editing, working with Ghanaian artists like Kofi Kinaata, Tinny and Kurl Songx.

Family Legacy

Jerry Bampoe Addo

My father, my hero: a Highlife guitar legend who played for Black Beats (1952) and Rambler's International Dance Band (1961), and played lead guitar on "Oman Bo Adwo" by Nana Ampadu (1970).

My Teacher

Isaac Alotey aka African Lion

"He unveiled the dancer in me." National Champion 2002, Malta Guinness 2003, and founder of Dancefrica Ghana and Dancefrica USA. Currently based in the USA.
